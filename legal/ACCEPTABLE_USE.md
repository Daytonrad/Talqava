# Acceptable Use Policy

*Effective 24 November 2026*

Talqava lets you run AI agents that act for your organization. Use them responsibly. This policy is part of our [Terms of Use](TERMS.md).

## Do not use Talqava or its agents to

- break the law, or help anyone else break it;
- harm, threaten, harass, stalk or discriminate against anyone;
- send spam, or unsolicited messages in bulk;
- deceive people: impersonate a person or organization, fake reviews or records, or hide that a message was written by AI where the law or the other side's terms require you to say so;
- collect or use people's personal information without a lawful basis, or in breach of privacy laws;
- infringe anyone's copyright, trademarks or other rights;
- write, spread or run malware, or break into, overload or damage systems you are not authorized to test;
- get around security, rate limits, paywalls or usage limits of any service;
- make decisions that seriously affect people (such as credit, employment, housing, insurance, legal or medical matters) without meaningful human review.

## Follow the services you connect

Your AI model provider, GitHub and every connector have their own usage policies. When an agent uses a service, that service's policy applies too, and it may stop serving you if it is broken.

## Keep a human in charge

Leave approvals on for anything that sends messages, spends money or changes things others depend on. Check agents' work before it reaches customers, staff or the public.

## Reporting misuse

If you see Talqava used against this policy, or find a way it could be, tell us through [talqava.com](https://talqava.com). Security problems go through our [Security policy](../SECURITY.md).
