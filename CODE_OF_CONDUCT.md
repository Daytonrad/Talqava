# Code of Conduct

Talqava is built for, and with, people of every background and every level of technical skill. We want taking part to be welcoming and safe for everyone.

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new, or not technical.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Disagree about ideas, never attack people.
- Give and take feedback gracefully, and own your mistakes.
- Put what is best for the people who use Talqava first.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity or background.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where it applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews), in any Talqava community space, and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainers privately through [talqava.com](https://talqava.com), or through GitHub's private **Report a vulnerability** form on the [Security tab](https://github.com/Talqava/Talqava/security) if that is easier. Do not use public issues. Every report is read, kept confidential, and answered.

## What happens next

The maintainers decide what to do, in proportion: a private word, a public warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent one. People who break this code in a project space may have their comments, commits or issues removed.
